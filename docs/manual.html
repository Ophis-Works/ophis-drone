ophis-drone — user manual

1. What it is

ophis-drone is a drone synthesizer. Three oscillator chains drone continuously; each has its own sub-oscillators, envelope, filter and sequencer, and any chain can modulate any other through a 16-slot patch matrix. There are no musical notes or scales — every pitch is a plain frequency in Hz, so you tune the interval between the chains by ear.

The same program runs on a Linux desktop, on the R36S handheld and on the RG28XX handheld, with identical features on all three.

2. Installing

Grab a package from the releases page. Every package includes the factory presets and the manual.

PackageWhere it goes

(Linux x86-64)		 Unpack anywhere and run ophis-drone-desktop/ophis-drone. Needs SDL2 and ALSA (installed on nearly every Linux desktop).
ophis-drone-r36s.zip
(R36S, ArkOS)		 Unzip into the roms/ partition of the SD card, so a ports/ folder appears next to your other ROM folders. Restart EmulationStation and launch "Ophis Drone" from the Ports menu.
ophis-drone-rg28xx.zip
(RG28XX)		 Extract at the root of the ROMS partition. Launch from the Ports menu.

Presets are always saved to a presets/ folder next to the program itself, wherever you unpacked or installed it.

3. The screen

The screen has a fixed layout:

  • Status strip (top): RUN state, gate level, and the OPHIS DRONE title. TRIGGER and PANIC flash briefly here.
  • Tab bar: OSC 1 | OSC 2 | OSC 3 | MOD | MASTER. On the OSC tabs a second bar shows the sub-tabs OSC | ENV+FLT | SEQ. The OSC tabs also show a live envelope bar (ENV) for the chain you are looking at.
  • Content: the parameters of the current tab, grouped under gray section labels. A highlighted row is the cursor.
  • Hint bar (bottom): the keys that do what, on your machine, right now.

On a SEQ page the eight sequencer steps are drawn as rectangles: filled when the step's velocity is above zero, empty when silent. The selected step has a thick border; the step currently playing has an orange dot.

Hold Z/X (or Y/X) down to repeat a value change. Value changes do not retrigger the sound — only RUN, TRIGGER and sequencer steps do.

Inside any menu: Enter/A selects, Esc (desktop) or B (handheld) cancels. On the desktop, Esc is the only way to close a menu; Space and T do nothing inside menus.

5. The OSC tabs (one per chain)

Each chain — A (OSC 1), B (OSC 2), C (OSC 3) — has three sub-tabs.

5.1 OSC sub-tab

RowWhat it does
FREQThe oscillator's pitch, 0.01 Hz – 20 kHz. Shown as a digit box like 00055.50 Hz: Left/Right move the highlighted digit, Z/X step that digit. Dimmed for the noise waves (noise has no pitch).
WAVEsine · tri · saw · square · white · pink. The saw and square are band-limited — they stay smooth and clean at any pitch.
DUTYSquare pulse width, 5–95 %. Only active when WAVE is square. Narrow pulses sound thinner and brighter.
LEVELThe chain's volume, 0–100 %.
VELThe loudness of each retrigger (TRIGGER key, or a sequencer step hitting the chain), 0–100 %.

Below MAIN come two identical SUB 1 / SUB 2 sections. Each sub-oscillator is a second voice playing the main pitch divided down:

RowWhat it does
DIV1–16. The sub plays FREQ / DIV — /1 is unison, /2 an octave down, /3 a fifth-ish down, and so on.
WAVEsine · tri · saw · square (subs are tonal — no noise).
DUTYPulse width when the sub's wave is square.
LEVELHow loud the sub sits in the chain, 0–100 %. At 0 the sub is silent — but it still works as a modulator source (see MOD).

5.2 ENV+FLT sub-tab

The ADSR section shapes the chain's loudness over time:

RowWhat it does
ATTACKTime to rise from silence to full level (0–60 s).
DECAYTime to fall from full level to the sustain level.
SUSTAINLevel held while the chain is on, 0–100 %.
RELEASETime to fall to silence after the chain is turned off (0–60 s).

The FILTER section shapes the tone:

RowWhat it does
MODELP (bass through, treble cut) · HP · BP (band) · BR (notch).
CUTOFFThe filter's corner frequency, 20 Hz – 20 kHz. Lower = darker.
RESResonance 0–100 % — adds a whistle at the cutoff. High values are sharp; back off if it screams.
ENV−100..+100 %. The chain's own envelope sweeps the cutoff: positive = brighter at the peak of the envelope, negative = darker. Classic synth "pluck" comes from CUTOFF low, ENV high.
POLES12 dB/oct (gentle) or 24 dB/oct (steep).

5.3 SEQ sub-tab — the sequencer

One sequencer per chain, running only while RUN is ON:

RowWhat it does
SEQ ONTurns the sequencer for this chain on or off. With it off, the chain holds its FREQ (direct drone mode).
DIVStep speed: each step lasts 60/BPM/4 × DIV seconds (one 16th note at DIV /1). Shown /1../16.
MODEStep order: fwd 1→8 · pingpong up then down · oddeven 1,3,5,7 then 2,4,6,8 · random (never the same step twice in a row) · markov a ±1 random walk.
ONCEloop plays forever; once plays one pass and then waits — press RUN off/on to play it again.
STEPThe eight rectangles. Left/Right (from anywhere on the page) pick which step PITCH and VEL edit below.
PITCHThe selected step's frequency, 0.01 Hz – 20 kHz.
VELThe selected step's loudness, 0–100 %. At 0 the step is silent — the drone simply sustains, nothing restarts.

There is exactly one tempo for the whole synth: BPM on the MASTER tab. All three sequencers follow it.

6. The MOD tab — the patch matrix

This is where chains start interacting. Sixteen independent slots; each can listen to any chain's oscillator or sub-oscillator and do something to any chain. Edit one slot at a time:

RowWhat it does
SLOTWhich of the 16 slots you are editing (1–16). Left/ Right from any row rolls through the slots.
SOURCEWhat listens: OSC 1/2/3 or one of their six subs. "OSC 2 SUB 1" is chain B's first sub, and so on.
TARGETWho gets affected: OSC 1, 2 or 3.
DESTWhat happens at the target: PITCH (vibrato, depth in Hz) · PHASE · AMP (tremolo) · FLT CUT (filter sweep) · FLT RES (resonance sweep) · FLT ENV (sweeps the target's filter ENV amount) · RING (ring mod — metallic sidebands; depth = dry/wet) · FM (frequency modulation, up to wild) · ADSR A / D / S / R (modulates the target's envelope stage times or sustain).
DEPTHHow hard the source acts. In Hz for PITCH, percent for everything else. Dimmed while DEST is OFF.
LEVELThe slot's own ADD knob: blends the source's audio into the target, 0–100 %, independent of DEST. This is how one chain thickens another, or how you mix a sub in without turning its own LEVEL up.

Some starting points:

  • Slow vibrato: SRC = a chain with a low sine, DEST = PITCH, DEPTH 2–5 Hz.
  • Classic pluck on OSC 2: SRC = OSC 1, DEST = FLT CUT, DEPTH 50–100 %.
  • Evil metallic: SRC = OSC 2, DEST = RING, TARGET = OSC 1, DEPTH 50 %.
  • Thicken: SRC = OSC 2 SUB 1, DEST = OFF, LEVEL 40 % — pure audio layering, no modulation.

7. The MASTER tab

Sections MASTER, MIX, DELAY, REVERB:

RowWhat it does
MASTEROverall volume, 0–100 %.
SAFETYA fixed protective filter (deep bass and extreme treble rolled off), ON by default. Turn off only if you know why.
BPMThe global tempo, 1–500. All sequencers follow it.
OSC A / OSC B / OSC CEach chain's send into the master bus, 0–100 %. At 0 a chain is silent in the mix but still drives any mod slot as a source.
TIME LDelay length, 0–2 s (left channel).
FEEDBACKHow many echo repeats. Keep below ~80 % — it can build up fast.
TONEDarkness of the echoes, 20 Hz – 20 kHz. Low values = muffled, dub-style repeats.
MIXDry/wet blend of the delay, 0–100 %.
SIZEReverb space, 0–100 %.
DAMPHow fast the reverb absorbs high frequencies.
MIXDry/wet blend of the reverb.
WIDTHStereo spread of the reverb, 0–100 %.

The factory presets ship with all effects silent — everything above starts from a dry sound.

8. Transport: RUN, TRIGGER, PANIC

  • RUN starts and stops everything. ON: the gate opens with a short fade and all enabled sequencers run. OFF: everything releases through its envelope's RELEASE.
  • TRIGGER hits all three chains once at their VEL — a drum-hit for the drone, sequencers untouched. It works even while RUN is off, holding the sound until the envelopes die away.
  • PANIC (in the menu): instant silence and a full reset of every engine state. The escape hatch for "what did I just patch".

9. The menu

Esc (desktop) / Start (R36S) / M (RG28XX) opens it from any screen:

  • NEW — reset everything to the factory defaults (asks first).
  • SAVE — name and save the current sound. Desktop: type the name, Enter saves, Esc cancels, Backspace deletes. Handheld: pick letters from a grid — d-pad moves, X inserts, Y deletes, A saves, B cancels. Names are 1–32 characters of letters, digits, space, _ and -. The next SAVE pre-fills the last name you saved.
  • LOAD — a folder browser: *.json presets and subfolders, .. goes up. Confirm loads the sound in one go.
  • PANIC — instant silence (see above).
  • EXIT — quits after a one-second "QUITTING" flash. There is no quit key on the main screen.
  • BACK — close the menu.

10. Presets

A preset is one JSON file holding every parameter except RUN. They live in presets/ next to the program. The LOAD dialog can reach presets in subfolders anywhere on the device. Files that are malformed, from an incompatible version, or out of range are rejected whole — the running sound is never half-changed by a bad load. You can also edit preset files in any text editor: the format is documented by example in the factory presets shipped alongside.

11. Troubleshooting

No sound
Check MASTER level and the chain's LEVEL, OSC A/B/C sends, and that RUN is ON. A chain with SEQ ON but all step velocities at 0 stays silent between retriggers. PANIC resets everything if a patch went wrong.
Loud screech
Resonance high + ENV positive is the usual suspect. Lower RES or FLT CUT on the offending chain, or PANIC and reload.
Echoes won't stop growing
DELAY FEEDBACK near 100 %. Turn it down; TONE lower also tames the build-up.
Handheld: buttons do the wrong thing
The mappings come from the system's controller database. Make sure your firmware is current; the RG28XX menu key is the top M key.
Desktop: "ALSA open failed"
Another program is holding the audio device exclusively, or no ALSA device is configured. Close the other program or check your sound setup, then start again.
